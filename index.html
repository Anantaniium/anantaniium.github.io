ABOUT

Between discovery
and opportunity.

A research path shaped by curiosity about the universe—and by a parallel interest in expanding access and understanding how systems and policies works.

I am interested in two connected questions: how we understand the universe, and how more people can take part in that work.

I am currently a Master's student in the Paris Physics Master at Université Paris Cité. My research experience spans Fast Radio Burst scintillation analysis at the California Institute of Technology, cosmological modelling at Miranda House, and gamma-ray source studies at the AstroParticle and Cosmology Laboratory in Paris.

Alongside research, I work on the educational and institutional pathways through which students encounter physics. I led the Indian physics student community and carried out major reforms to establish the Indian Network of Physics Students (INPS) and have worked on student-led conferences, academic exchange, science outreach and educational initiatives in India. As Vice President & Outreach and Advocacy Manager of IAPS, I contribute to international advocacy, outreach, student representation and cooperation across the wider scientific community. I aim to better understand the different challenges faced by physics students in different regions of the world, advocate for them and help build sustainable student communities, increase access to resources and opportunity, and drive more public engagement and outreach in physics.

These experiences have shaped my interest in physics education research, science communication, science diplomacy and the role of science in public policy. I am interested not only in how scientific knowledge is produced, but also in how it is taught, shared and made accessible.

I hope to contribute to stronger educational pathways, teaching methods and institutions that connect scientific talent with opportunity, particularly in communities and regions that remain underrepresented in international science.

01
MAY—JULY 2026PARIS, FRANCE

Statistical Validation of Two Ultra-High-Energy Gamma-Ray Candidate Sources

AstroParticle and Cosmology (APC) Laboratory · Université Paris Cité

Supervised by Prof. Dmitri Semikoz & Zhanfeng Zhu

We did statistical validation of two ultra-high-energy (UHE, greater than 100 TeV) gamma-ray candidate sources from the LHAASO catalog. Using gammapy and Monte Carlo simulations to benchmark Sommers' theoretical exposure model and observed data against Monte Carlo background simulations. Evaluated statistical significance across the 3 σ–4 σ regime, distinguishing genuine high-energy emission candidates from background fluctuations.

02
UNDERGRADUATE RESEARCHNEW DELHI, INDIA

Joint analysis of FRB with SNe Ia to constrain H0 and compare DDE models

Miranda House · University of Delhi

Supervised by Dr. Abha Dev Habib & Dr. Nisha Rani

Using the DMIGMsub - redshift relation of localized Fast Radio Bursts to determine the value of Hubble constant. Constructed joint likelihoods with Pantheon+ SNe Ia to rigorously quantify the statistical weight of radio probes. Generated posterior contour plots demonstrating how FRBs break parameter degeneracies and significantly tighten constraints. Developed a JAX-based Bayesian pipeline to make the code efficient.

CosmologyFRBsDark energy
03
JUNE—AUGUST 2023PASADENA, USA

Fast Radio Burst scintillation analysis

California Institute of Technology · Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship (SURF) 2023

Supervised by Prof. E. Sterl Phinney & Dr. Dongzi Li

As a Caltech SURF Fellow in 2023, developed data-analysis pipelines to measure Fast Radio Burst scintillations for some of the brightest FRBs using CHIME/FRB Catalog 1 data. Results comparison with the scintillation model for the Milky Way to constrain the origin of scattering of FRBs.

FOR EVENT ORGANISERS & EDITORSBIO / 01

Official press bio

A concise biography for event programmes, institutional introductions and speaker announcements.

Anant Singh is a Master's student in Physics at Université Paris Cité and an astrophysics researcher whose work has included Fast Radio Burst scintillation analysis, cosmological modelling, parameter estimation and gamma-ray source studies. He currently serves as Vice President & Outreach and Advocacy Manager of the International Association of Physics Students (IAPS), and also the Chairperson of the Governing Council of the Indian Network of Physics Students (INPS). His work spans astrophysics, physics education, science communication and international scientific cooperation. Alongside, he is also building his personal science communication platform @anantaniium while also shaping ARYUK - a platform for creating grassroots impact in education for school students.
